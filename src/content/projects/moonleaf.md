summary: "A free Android app that **downloads web novels and builds them into EPUB 3 books on the phone**, then reads them in a built-in reader modeled on KOReader. Paste a novel's page or any chapter link and it finds the book and fetches it from chapter 1, from **six sources** including Royal Road, Scribble Hub, FanFiction.net and AO3. Sites behind a Cloudflare human check load in a **hidden WebView**, which surfaces the check only when it won't pass on its own and then downloads at a gentle one page every 1.5 seconds. The library follows Mihon's layout with Plan to read, Reading and Completed shelves, and the reader covers paginated or scrolling view, seven page themes including E-ink, highlights with notes, full-text search and reading statistics. **No account, ads or tracking**: the library and progress stay on the device, and every merge publishes a checksummed APK through GitHub Actions."
